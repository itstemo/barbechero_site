Barbechero is a family project in mezcal and agave spirit. Every lot on
this site — its agave, its place, its maestro mezcalero — is documented as
it is: no embellishment, no marketing promises.

We are a union of two families with a background in agriculture and a
love of agaves. We aim to preserve and highlight the beauty of the agave
plant, its resilience, and the work of the growers and distillers who
work the fields and distill the agave.

This project started in 2020. We've slowly planted around 20 hectares of
different agave species across two states, Oaxaca and Guerrero, and we
mean to keep planting more — endemic and wild agaves, alongside Espadín
and hybrids of Agave angustifolia.

We hope to keep experimenting: with different agaves, different
distillation methods, and both scientific and artisanal ways of crafting
unique expressions. Each bottle is unique, and highlights its own taste
profile.

On this site you'll find the details behind every lot we make —
photographs, interviews, videos, and an explanation of what each batch
is.

Barbecho is to till the earth and let it rest. That is how we work.
